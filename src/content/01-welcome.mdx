## What Is a Newsletter, _really_?

At its core, a newsletter is:

1. **Content**: written in some format (Markdown, HTML, Text, MDX)
2. **Rendering**: turned into email-safe HTML (using [React Email](https://react.email/))
3. **Delivery**: sent via an email service provider (like [Resend](https://resend.com/)!)

Now, let me tell you about **[MDX](https://mdxjs.com/)**.

## Why MDX?

> **MDX** = **Markdown** + **JSX**.

MDX allows you to include [React](https://react.dev/)/[Vue](https://vuejs.org/) components when you need them.

```md
# Welcome to the newsletter

Here's a **paragraph**.

// Custom component ⬇️
<SubscribeButton plan="premium" />

And _back_ to normal text.
```

### Benefits for newsletters:

1. **Familiar syntax**: you might already know Markdown
0. **Integrates well with AI**: you can ask AI to generate components while you focus on the actual content
0. **Dynamic components**: stand out from other newsletters with custom components
0. **Preview on web**: same components render on your site AND in emails
